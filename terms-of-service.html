2. Software Subscriptions, Tiers & Financial Terms

Our software suites operate on transparent subscription models:

If student enrollment expands beyond the contracted threshold, the institution agrees to upgrade to the appropriate tier.

  • Currency & Invoicing: All subscription charges are invoiced in Uganda Shillings (UGX) unless otherwise agreed in writing. Invoices are payable via bank transfer, school fees collection accounts, or verified Telecom Mobile Money merchant lines.
  • Grace Window: As a commitment to our partners, SmartSchool.ug grants a 90-day post-activation grace window to enable schools to collect termly fees before final settlement deadlines.
  • Taxes: Unless explicitly stated, pricing excludes statutory value-added tax or electronic payment transaction levies required by the Uganda Revenue Authority (URA).
