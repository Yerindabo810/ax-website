FAQs
1. How does the 60-minute Attendance Edit window work?

You can edit taken attendance within the first 60 minutes. After 60 minutes, it locks and the next roll call is saved as a new attendance session.

2. Are my Timetable lessons saved in the main system?

Yes! Lessons you add on the mobile app are saved directly into the main school timetable and you can add multiple lessons on the same day.

3. What happens when I am offline?

If your internet connection drops, the app immediately alerts you to reconnect to Wi-Fi or Mobile Data.
