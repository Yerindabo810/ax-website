Frequently Asked Questions

Does SmartSchool.ug support both Primary and Secondary schools?

Yes. SmartSchool.ug is built specifically for Ugandan schools, supporting Nursery/Primary grading schemes, the New Lower Secondary CBC Competency-Based Curriculum, and A-Level subject combinations.

What is included in the annual fee?

Every tier includes dedicated isolated database hosting, a unique school subdomain portal, automated CBC/UNEB grading engines, printable termly report cards with verification QR codes, and bursar receipts.

How do we get started?

Simply contact us through our website, WhatsApp, or phone. We will provision your portal, upload your student biodata, and conduct training for your bursar and teaching staff.
