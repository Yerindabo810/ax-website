School & Vocational Capabilities

Tailored for Secondary & Vocational School Workflows

Secondary and vocational institutions manage specialized operations: trade workshops, practical craft assessments, CBC secondary curricula, UBTEB/UNEB modular grading, boarding houses, and student self-service. CampusDesk brings all these departments into one fast, intuitive platform.

Multi-Campus & Branch Sync

Manage main secondary school campuses, annexes, and vocational training centers from a central dashboard with scoped headteacher, principal, and department permissions.

Secondary & Vocational Grading

Configurable grading engine supporting Secondary CBC (Lower Secondary AoI & Summative), A-Level combinations, and Vocational/BTVET modular assessments & practicals.

Student & Staff Web Portals

24/7 mobile-responsive web portals for students and trainees to check termly report cards, results, class timetables, and fee statements directly on smartphones.

Bursar Fees & Mobile Money

Automated school fees billing by class, vocational trade, or boarding status. Instant automated SMS receipts, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and bank deposit reconciliations.

Biometric & QR Attendance

Biometric fingerprint readers and QR student identity cards for classroom roll call, vocational workshops, laboratory sessions, and examination room clearance.

Dormitories & Boarding Houses

Real-time bed space tracking across boarding houses and dormitories, automated boarding fee billing, warden shift logs, and room inventory control.

Library & Workshop Registry

ISBN cataloging and textbook circulation, combined with vocational workshop equipment, apparatus lending, and trade tools inventory.

Enterprise Security & Compliance

Compliant with Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, granular role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption, and daily automated off-site cloud backups.

Deep-Dive Architecture

Complete Secondary & Vocational Modules Included

Everything a secondary school headteacher, Director of Studies (DOS), vocational institute principal, and bursar need without third-party plugins.

Admissions & Student Registry

  • Secondary & vocational trainee intake registration
  • Automatic registration & UNEB/UBTEB index numbering
  • Digital student record dossiers & PLE/UCE certificate uploads
  • Instant batch student ID cards generation with QR code

Director of Studies (DOS) & Exam Engine

  • Secondary subject combinations & vocational craft courses
  • Continuous Coursework assessments, workshop practicals & exams
  • Instant automated CBC/UBTEB grading and termly reports
  • Official academic report cards, result slips & completion certificates

Bursar Financial Ledgers & Payments

  • Tiered fees by class, vocational trade & boarding section
  • Real-time payment reconciliation (MTN MoMo, Airtel, Banks)
  • Instant thermal or A4 receipts with verification QR codes
  • Real-time fee defaulters lists & automated parent SMS reminders

Boarding Houses & Dormitories

  • House, wing, room, and bed occupancy status
  • Automated boarding billing integrated into student fee ledger
  • Housemaster / warden incident logging, visitor sign-in, and roll call
  • Bed maintenance, beginning-of-term & end-of-term room audits

Teacher & Instructor Workload

  • Secondary teacher & vocational instructor timetable allocation
  • Staff profile registry, certifications & contracts
  • Marks entry portal with deadline locks per term/semester
  • Monthly staff payroll preparation with NSSF and PAYE

Exam Clearance & Biometric Attendance

  • Automated examination eligibility cards with student photo
  • Zero-fee balance clearance checks prior to exam permit issuance
  • Hardware integration with USB / LAN biometric fingerprint readers
  • Live attendance dashboards by class stream & workshop section
