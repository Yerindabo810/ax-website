Authentic School Modules

Purpose-Built for Day-to-Day Operations in Uganda

CampusDesk is built around the authentic administrative and academic hierarchy of Ugandan secondary and vocational schools—from Headteacher document approvals, DOS exam management, and Bursar SchoolPay reconciliations, to Sickbay clinics, Dean passouts, and dual Student/Parent portals.

Vocational Courses & Trades

Dedicated vocational course catalog, technical instructor workload assignment, student trade enrollments, and practical workshop skills tracking for vocational institutions and technical secondary schools.

SchoolPay & Fees Invoicing

Integrated SchoolPay student payment code synchronization, Day/Boarding/New student fee structures, partial/full bursary scholarships, receipting, and instant WhatsApp fee reminders to defaulters.

CBC & A-Level Exam Engine

Formative AOI (Activities of Integration 20%) & Summative (BOT/EOT 80%) Lower Secondary CBC grading, A-Level subject points (A=6 down to F=0), UNEB LINs tracking, and cumulative bi-weekly tests.

Digital Schemes of Work

Teachers draft weekly schemes of work online for departmental submission. DOS and Headteacher review, approve, or reject with comments, and export official printable PDFs.

Dean Passouts & Discipline

Student permission slips and gate passouts with QR approval, suspension registers, behavior incident logging, counseling files, student rewards, prefect categories, and co-curricular club rosters.

School Sickbay & Clinic

Managed by the school nurse: student health profiles, visit timestamps, admission/discharge logs, and medication dispensing that automatically deducts dispensary drug stocks.

Uniforms & Term Requirements

Track school uniform issuance and inventory per learner. Manage termly physical requirements (reams of paper, brooms, hoes, soap) with instant submission checklists.

Dual Student & Parent Portals

Independent self-service portals for learners and parents. View real-time attendance, fee statements, e-lessons, download report card PDFs, and print parent portal access cards.

Role-Based System Architecture

Complete Workflows by Department

Every user role in the school possesses a specialized, security-scoped workspace tailored to their exact daily duties.

Headteacher & Administration

  • Executive dashboard: expected vs. collected fees, outstanding arrears
  • Financial document approvals: school budgets and requisitions
  • Digital scheme of work authorization and review
  • Real-time read-only oversight of Dean discipline and nurse sickbay logs

Director of Studies (DOS)

  • Configurable grade scales (O-Level CBC & A-Level Principal/Sub)
  • Exam permit generation with automated clearance serial numbers
  • Freeze and lock exam marks entry post-deadline
  • Digital signature management for automated report card printing
  • Class marksheets, UNEB LINs tracking, and performance analytics

Teachers & Technical Instructors

  • Formative AOIs (a1–a4) entry scaled out of 20% for Lower Secondary
  • Summative BOT & EOT marks entry scaled out of 80%
  • Cumulative bi-weekly test scores and CAI competency logs
  • CBC student project assessment and score exports
  • Daily lesson attendance registers and virtual E-lesson links

Bursar & School Finance

  • SchoolPay automated bank payment sync and verification
  • Instant fee receipts with verification QR codes and WhatsApp receipts
  • Bulk WhatsApp fee reminders sent directly to parents with balance
  • Staff monthly payroll: PAYE tax brackets, NSSF, allowances & bank slips
  • Meal card generation and printing for cafeteria control

Dean, Matron & Boarding Wardens

  • Electronic permission slips and student passouts (gate passes)
  • Student personal property registry recorded by Matron & Warden
  • Suspensions, student conduct points, and counseling interview logs
  • Hostel and dormitory bed assignments & School Families system

Sickbay Clinic & Library

  • Clinic visits, student allergies, vitals, and bed admissions
  • Drug dispensing with automated clinic inventory stock-out
  • Library book cataloging, barcode scanning, and borrowing logs
  • Overdue return tracking and member circulation records
Academic Verification

Verifiable Report Cards & WhatsApp Integration

CampusDesk report cards come with cryptographic QR codes that link directly to the live verification engine (/verify/report-card), preventing grade alteration or forgery.

Automated WhatsApp Delivery

Parents receive official termly report cards and instant fee payment receipts directly on their WhatsApp phone numbers via the automated webhook system.

Instant QR Authenticity Scan

Employers, higher institutions, and sponsors can scan the QR code printed on the report card or student ID to instantly verify grades and enrollment status.

Certificate Generator

Built-in custom certificate generator for school completion, vocational trade mastery, academic excellence, leadership, and sports honors.

Built-In AI Assistant

Integrated AI assistant to help teachers formulate comments, analyze student performance trends, and assist school staff with report summaries.
