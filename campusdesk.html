Institutional Capabilities

Engineered for Complex Higher Education Operations

Higher institutions face multi-departmental complexity: modular semesters, varying tuition rates, multi-branch satellite administration, and student self-service. CampusDesk unites every workflow under one high-speed platform.

Multi-Campus Centralization

Manage multiple campuses, branch centers, and affiliate schools from a unified super-admin dashboard with scoped campus deans and departmental autonomy.

Semester GPA & CGPA Engine

Fully configurable credit units, core and elective course weightings, continuous coursework marks, retake tracking, and automated transcript compilation.

Student & Faculty Web Portals

24/7 responsive web portals for students to register course units, check fee ledgers, access result slips, and submit clearance forms from their phones.

Bursar Fees & Mobile Money

Automated tuition billing by intake and study mode (Day/Evening/Weekend). Instant automated receipts, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and bank slip tracking.

Biometric & QR Attendance

Biometric fingerprint scanning and QR code card verification for lecture hall attendance, library access, and 75% examination eligibility clearance.

Hostels & Hall Allocation

Real-time bed space tracking across residential halls and partnered hostels, automated boarding invoices, warden shift logs, and room inventory control.

Library Management & Barcode

ISBN cataloging, book lending/return automation with barcode scanners, digital repository indexing, and overdue fine computation.

Enterprise Security & Compliance

Compliant with Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, granular role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption, and daily automated off-site cloud backups.
